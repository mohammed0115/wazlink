# B7 — Frontend Behavior Inventory

> **B7 status:** Target design only. Not closed. Awaits independent CTO verification.

## 1. Scope and method

Inspected: `client/src/features/automation/{Automation.tsx,AutomationModal.tsx,Appointments.tsx,AppointmentModal.tsx,Tasks.tsx}`, `client/src/domain/automation.js`, `client/src/domain/data.js` (automation catalogs and mock model, lines 776-996), `BACKEND_PUBLIC_ID_REGISTRY.md` (the frozen registry's own classification of every automation-shaped identifier). No other feature folder (`crm/`, `sales/`, `inbox/`, `intelligence/`, `discovery/`, `dashboard/`, `analytics/`) contains automation-specific logic beyond the `createdByAutomationRunId` provenance field surfaced on Task/Appointment rows (FB-D21) — confirmed by targeted grep across those folders for `automation|workflow|rule|trigger|condition|action|schedule`.

The automation feature is explicitly self-documented in its own source as **"S9 — deterministic in-session simulation, not a Scheduler, Worker, or Queue"** (`Automation.tsx:1-7`, `automation.js:1`, `data.js:776`). This is the single most important piece of evidence in this inventory (FB-D23): it tells the reader, in the frozen frontend's own words, which parts of the mock are business-authoritative and which are mechanism artifacts of a synchronous demo that must not be carried into a real asynchronous backend.
