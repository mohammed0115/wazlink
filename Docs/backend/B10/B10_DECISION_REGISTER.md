# B10 — Decision Register

> `CLASS_A_UNRESOLVED = 0`. Every Class A decision below has an enforcement artifact and acceptance evidence. Two competent engineers reading this register plus the documents it cites should derive identical behavior without inventing a rule.

## Class A — must be resolved before implementation

| ID | Question | Decision | Reason | Enforcement artifact | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `B10-D-A001` | Who owns tax/ZATCA configuration — Workspace, LegalEntity, Billing account, or Platform operator? | A new `LegalEntity` aggregate, **platform-scoped, not workspace-scoped**; `TaxProfile` is a versioned child of `LegalEntity`. Phase 1 seeds exactly one `LegalEntity` row (the current WazLink operating entity) | ZATCA applicability is a property of the **seller** (WazLink, issuing subscriptions to many workspaces), not of any individual buyer/workspace; attaching it to Workspace would break the moment one legal entity owns many workspaces, and attaching it to Billing/Subscription would duplicate identity across every renewal | `B10_LEGAL_ENTITY_TAX_PROFILE.md`, `B10_DOMAIN_MODEL.md` §2 | `AT-B10LE-1`, `AT-B10LE-2` |
| `B10-D-A002` | What are the explicit ZATCA applicability states, and what is the default? | Five states: `unknown` (default at bootstrap) → `not_applicable` \| `applicable_not_enabled` → `enabled` ⇄ `suspended`. A single boolean is rejected as insufficient (loses "not legally applicable" vs "applicable but not yet enabled" vs "undetermined") | Brief §4 mandates this distinction explicitly; collapsing it into a boolean cannot represent "we haven't decided yet" without an unsafe default | `B10_TAX_APPLICABILITY_MODEL.md` §2 | `AT-B10APP-1`…`5` |
| `B10-D-A003` | What happens when applicability is `unknown`? | Commerce (B8 payments/subscriptions/entitlements) is **never** blocked by B10 — B10 has no read gate on any B8 command. Only `IssueTaxInvoice` is deferred: the underlying B8 `InvoiceIssued` fact is retained on an admin-visible pending-classification backlog, never silently dropped, never silently issued as if `not_applicable`, never silently issued as if `enabled` | Brief §5 forbids both silent defaults; financial continuity must not depend on a compliance decision no one has made yet | `B10_TAX_APPLICABILITY_MODEL.md` §3, `B10_INVOICE_MODEL.md` §4 | `AT-B10APP-2`, `AT-B10APP-3` |
| `B10-D-A004` | Does B10 own an `Invoice`/`TaxInvoice`/`CreditNote`/`DebitNote` model, and how do the document shapes map to storage? | `TaxInvoice` (frozen aggregate, `TAX-*` prefix, frozen `tax_invoices`/`tax_invoice_lines`/`tax_submissions` tables) hosts every document shape across **two independent columns** (corrected under `B10-FIX.1`, closes `B10-AUDIT-C1`/`D1`): `document_kind` (`invoice`\|`credit_note`\|`debit_note`, renamed from a single four-value `document_type`) and `invoice_classification` (`standard`\|`simplified`, new); credit/debit notes carry a mandatory self-referencing `reference_invoice_id` and inherit `invoice_classification` from it (§`B10_INVOICE_MODEL.md` §1a). No new table, no new public-ID prefix | ZATCA itself treats notes as structural variants of the same UBL invoice schema referencing an original; splitting into a separate `credit_notes` table would duplicate every column this document set already needs and widen the controlled-amendment surface for no architectural gain | `B10_INVOICE_MODEL.md` §1–2, `B10_CREDIT_NOTE_MODEL.md` §1, `B10_STORAGE_MODEL.md` | `AT-B10INV-1`, `AT-B10CN-1` |
| `B10-D-A005` | Is a WazLink commercial `Invoice` (B8-owned, `INV-BILL-*`) the same thing as a `TaxInvoice` (B10-owned, `TAX-*`)? | No. `Invoice` is the commercial billing document (what a subscription was charged); `TaxInvoice` is the statutory tax document, produced **only** when `TaxProfile.zatca_applicability ∈ {applicable_not_enabled, enabled, suspended}`, referencing the `Invoice` by `source_type="b8_invoice"`/`source_ref=INV-BILL-*`. Under `not_applicable`, no `TaxInvoice` is ever created — `Invoice` alone remains the customer's document | Frozen `BACKEND_BILLING_TAX_ARCHITECTURE.md` "Invoice and TaxInvoice are separate" predates B10; conflating them would force ZATCA machinery onto every payment regardless of applicability, violating brief §2's explicit prohibition | `B10_B8_BILLING_BOUNDARY.md` §2–3 | `AT-B10INV-2`, `AT-B10APP-1` |
| `B10-D-A006` | Is invoice content immutable after issuance, and how are errors corrected? | Once `status=issued`, every content field (seller/buyer snapshot, lines, totals, tax amounts, document type, issue timestamp) is immutable. Corrections are **always** a new `CreditNote`/`DebitNote` referencing the original — never an `UPDATE` to an issued row. Only `status` itself may still transition (`issued→cancelled` in a narrow pre-acceptance window; `issued→credited` once fully offset) | A statutory tax document silently rewritten after issuance is indefensible under any e-invoicing regime and destroys the audit trail brief §12 requires | `B10_INVOICE_STATE_MACHINE.md` §1, `B10_STORAGE_MODEL.md` `tax_invoices` mutability column | `AT-B10INV-3`, `AT-B10CN-2` |
| `B10-D-A007` | When is the tax fact set (rates, seller/buyer identity, applicability) snapshotted onto a document? | At `IssueTaxInvoice` time, from the `TaxProfile` version that was current (`effective_to IS NULL`) at that instant, copied onto the row as typed columns — never a live join to mutable configuration read later. `tax_profile_version_id` pins the snapshot | Brief §29 forbids historical interpretation depending on current mutable settings; mirrors `B8-D-A003`'s `PlanVersion` precedent exactly | `B10_LEGAL_ENTITY_TAX_PROFILE.md` §3, `B10_STORAGE_MODEL.md` | `AT-B10APP-4` |
| `B10-D-A008` | Does changing applicability retroactively rewrite historical documents? | Never. A `TaxApplicabilityChanged` transition affects only documents issued **after** its `effective_from`; every already-issued `TaxInvoice` keeps the `tax_profile_version_id` snapshot it was issued under | Brief §28 explicitly forbids retroactive rewriting absent an explicit legal migration workflow, which is not designed in Phase 1 (`B10-D-B002`) | `B10_TAX_APPLICABILITY_MODEL.md` §5 | `AT-B10APP-4` |
| `B10-D-A009` | How does B10 relate to B8's `Refund`? | `Refund` (B8) is never automatically a `CreditNote` (B10). B8 exposes no produced event for refunds in its frozen `B8_COMMAND_EVENT_CATALOG.md`, so B10 never "consumes" a refund event — `CreateCreditNote` is **always** an explicit, reason-required operator command, manually correlated by the operator viewing the B8 refund, never system-triggered from it | Brief §20 forbids automatic equivalence; inventing a B8 consumed-event that does not exist in the frozen catalog would itself be a frozen-artifact violation | `B10_B8_BILLING_BOUNDARY.md` §4, `B10_CREDIT_NOTE_MODEL.md` §2 | `AT-B10REF-1` |
| `B10-D-A010` | Does a `CreditNote` automatically create a B9 `RevenueReversal`? | No. `CreditNoteIssued` (B10 event) is a fact only. B9 owns 100% of the decision to reverse recognized revenue, under its own frozen authority; B10 mints no B9 command and writes no B9 table | Symmetric with `B9_B10_TAX_BOUNDARY.md` §5's own "a reversal is a credit note" non-inference; B9 is frozen and B10 must not assume a consumer that does not exist | `B10_B9_FINANCE_BOUNDARY.md` §2–3 | `AT-B10REV-1` |
| `B10-D-A011` | What is ZATCA submission authority — who/what may submit a document to ZATCA? | Only the system-actor `SubmitTaxDocumentForProcessing` command, itself gated on `TaxProfile.zatca_applicability = enabled`, may invoke the `TaxProvider` port. No manual "submit now" bypass exists outside `RetryTaxSubmission` (transport retry only, never a new document) | Brief §15/§24 require a single owning-domain workflow, not ad hoc submission paths that could double-submit | `B10_ZATCA_BOUNDARY.md` §2, `B10_COMMAND_EVENT_CATALOG.md` | `AT-B10ZAT-1`…`3` |
| `B10-D-A012` | Who owns ZATCA credentials/CSID, and how are they stored? | Environment/secret management only, referenced by opaque pointer on `tax_profiles` (`credential_ref`, `environment`); never a database plaintext column, never logged, never returned in any API response. Mirrors `B8-D-A016`'s `payment.manage`-style posture, gated behind the new `zatca.manage` permission | Brief §17 treats signing credentials as high sensitivity; reusing the B8 secret-handling doctrine avoids inventing a second standard | `B10_ZATCA_SECURITY_CREDENTIALS.md` | `AT-B10SEC-1`…`4` |
| `B10-D-A013` | What is B10's idempotency model for issuance/submission? | `IssueTaxInvoice` is idempotent on `(source_type, source_ref)` — one `TaxInvoice` per underlying B8 `Invoice`, enforced by a partial unique index. `SubmitTaxDocumentForProcessing`/`RetryTaxSubmission` are idempotent on `(tax_invoice_id, attempt scope)` — a transport retry never creates a second `TaxInvoice`, only a new `tax_submissions` row | Brief §22 requires distinguishing business-document identity from transport-retry identity; conflating them risks a duplicate statutory document from a mere network timeout | `B10_IDEMPOTENCY_CONCURRENCY.md` §1–2 | `AT-B10IDEM-1`…`3` |
| `B10-D-A014` | What is B10's concurrency model for double-issuance/double-correction? | Durable DB uniqueness (partial unique indexes), never a Redis lock, exactly as `B8-D-A021`/`C11` established. `(source_type, source_ref) WHERE document_kind = 'invoice'` is unique (renamed from `document_type` under `B10-FIX.1`); two concurrent `IssueTaxInvoice` calls for the same `Invoice` race the constraint, loser gets `409 CONFLICT`. **Extended under `B10-FIX.1` (`B10-D-A021`):** double-*correction* (over-correction) is bounded by a row lock on the original plus a recomputed `net_corrected_total` check, not identity uniqueness alone — see `B10_CREDIT_NOTE_MODEL.md` §6 | Brief §23 forbids relying solely on Redis for financial-truth-adjacent uniqueness | `B10_IDEMPOTENCY_CONCURRENCY.md` §3, `B10_STORAGE_MODEL.md` | `AT-B10CONC-1`, `AT-B10CONC-2`, `AT-B10CORR-5` |
| `B10-D-A015` | What happens to a future applicability change from `not_applicable`/`unknown` to `applicable_*`? | A new `TaxProfile` version is created with a fresh `effective_from`; only `TaxInvoice`s issued at or after that instant follow the new rules. The pending-classification backlog (`B10-D-A003`) is the one exception: it is evaluated against whichever profile is current **when the backlog item is finally processed**, not backdated to when the original B8 `Invoice` was issued, and this asymmetry is recorded explicitly rather than left ambiguous | Brief §28 (high priority) requires an explicit answer, not an implied one; backdating a backlog item would itself be a retroactive rewrite of a decision that hadn't been made yet | `B10_TAX_APPLICABILITY_MODEL.md` §5–6 | `AT-B10APP-5` |
| `B10-D-A016` | Is applicability a normal-privilege or high-privilege action? | `tax.applicability.manage` (new, high privilege — Owner-only in Phase 1, no Admin `conditional` grant) is required to call `SetTaxApplicability`, distinct from the lower-privilege `tax.manage` (profile detail edits, credit notes, cancellations) | Brief §30 explicitly forbids a normal user marking "ZATCA exempt" merely to bypass integration; separating the permission is the only way to make that structurally true rather than a policy request | `B10_RBAC_TENANCY.md` §1–2 | `AT-B10RBAC-1`, `AT-B10RBAC-2` |
| `B10-D-A017` | Does an invoice need a separate "legal/human" number distinct from the WazLink public ID, and is it the same thing as ZATCA's ICV? | Yes to the first; **no** to the second (corrected under `B10-FIX.1`). `TAX-*` (public_id) remains the opaque, workspace-facing WazLink identity, reused verbatim, zero new prefix. A separate `legal_sequence_number` (nullable) is assigned only when `document_kind`/applicability requires it under an `applicable_*`/`enabled` profile, monotonic per `(legal_entity_id, environment)`, immutable once assigned, never reused, never assigned under `not_applicable`/`unknown`. It is a WazLink product/legal sequence field, **not** an assertion of ZATCA's Invoice Counter Value, which is scoped per EGS unit/production-CSID device — a concept B10 does not yet model (`B10-D-B008`, new) | Brief §18 requires the separation from the public ID; the original pass additionally, and incorrectly, implied `legal_sequence_number` *is* the ICV. Independent re-research (`B10-X-009` re-verified, `B10-X-015`, `B10_RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`) shows ICV is device/EGS-unit-scoped, not legal-entity-scoped, so asserting the two are the same field would freeze an unverified and plausibly-wrong mapping | `B10_INVOICE_MODEL.md` §3, `B10_STORAGE_MODEL.md` | `AT-B10NUM-1`, `AT-B10NUM-2` |
| `B10-D-A018` | How does B10 map real ZATCA Phase 1 (Generation) vs Phase 2 (Integration) onto the applicability states, and what determines the clearance-vs-reporting route for every document B10 can produce, including notes? | **Corrected under `B10-FIX.1` — this decision was falsely closed in the pre-FIX.1 pack and is re-resolved here, not left `UNRESOLVED`.** Phase mapping (unchanged): `applicable_not_enabled` = Phase-1-equivalent posture (generate + store locally, never transmitted, `B10-X-001`); `enabled` = Phase-2-equivalent posture (additionally submitted for clearance/reporting). **Routing (corrected):** the clearance-vs-reporting path is determined **solely by `invoice_classification` (`standard`\|`simplified`)** — a document property every `TaxInvoice` row carries (§`B10_INVOICE_MODEL.md` §1a) and, for `credit_note`/`debit_note`, inherits immutably from the `reference_invoice_id` row at creation. `document_kind` (invoice/credit_note/debit_note) is **never** an input to this decision. `standard` → clearance (real-time, pre-delivery); `simplified` → reporting (post-delivery, bounded window). This closes `B10-AUDIT-C1`: the pre-FIX.1 rule keyed routing on credit-vs-debit note type, which is not what any evidence — official or secondary — supports, and which the corrected model no longer permits even as a client input (no DTO field exists for a note to declare its own classification) | Brief §14/§6 forbid relying on outdated or invented assumptions; the corrected mapping is grounded in `B10-X-002` (re-verified) and the newly-added `B10-X-014` (UNCL1001 type-code/subtype structure), both rated `PARTIAL` — strengthened by multiple independently-concordant, technically-specific sources, but not promoted to `VERIFIED` because the official XML Implementation Standard PDF could not be rendered to readable text in this pass. This decision is resolved on the honest strength of that evidence, not by inflating its rating; the exact wire-level mechanics remain gated behind `B10-D-B001` | `B10_ZATCA_APPLICABILITY.md` §2–3, `B10_ZATCA_BOUNDARY.md` §3, `B10_DOMAIN_MODEL.md` §3 | `AT-B10ZAT-4`, `AT-B10CLASS-1`…`4` |
| `B10-D-A019` | What is the fail-closed rule for an unrecognized/ambiguous ZATCA submission outcome? | Maps to `zatca_status = pending` plus a mandatory reconciliation flag — never silently to `accepted`, never silently to `rejected`. Mirrors `B8`'s own provider-status fail-closed row (`B8_PAYMENT_PROVIDER_PORT.md` §3) exactly | Consistency with the corpus's established fail-closed doctrine for any third-party status the domain does not recognize | `B10_ZATCA_FAILURE_RETRY_MODEL.md` §2 | `AT-B10ZAT-5` |
| `B10-D-A020` | Does B10 require Discovery/Intelligence provenance for any invoice/tax behavior? | No. `TaxInvoice.source_ref` points only at a B8 `Invoice`, which itself never requires `DiscoveryJob`, Maps-sourced `Business`, or AI-intelligence provenance (already true of B8). Track A/Track B customers are indistinguishable to B10 | Brief §39 requires dual-track compatibility; B10 has no dependency to sever because it never had one | `B10_DUAL_TRACK_COMPATIBILITY.md` | `AT-B10DT-1` |

| `B10-D-A021` | (New, `B10-FIX.1`, closes `B10-AUDIT-N4`.) Can cumulative credit/debit notes against one original drive its net corrected total negative, and are credit and debit notes netted against each other for that purpose? | No — `net_corrected_total = original.total + SUM(debit notes) − SUM(credit notes)` must remain `≥ 0` after every commit, enforced by a row lock on the original plus a fresh recomputation before each new note's insert; credit and debit notes **are** netted for this bound's arithmetic (a debit note widens room for a later credit note) but each note remains its own independent, immutable, reason-carrying document | A product-level (not regulatory) sanity invariant: without it, two concurrent or sequential over-corrections could produce a nonsensical over-100%-credited state with no system-level signal | `B10_CREDIT_NOTE_MODEL.md` §6 | `AT-B10CORR-1`…`6` |
| `B10-D-A022` | (New, `B10-FIX.1`, closes `B10-AUDIT-M2`.) What is the durable model for a tax-document request received while `zatca_applicability = unknown`? | A dedicated `PendingTaxDocumentClassification` aggregate (`pending_tax_document_classifications` table), created by `IssueTaxInvoice` itself the moment it observes `unknown`, with four explicit states (`pending`\|`resolved_not_applicable`\|`resolved_for_issuance`\|`cancelled`), durable `(source_type, source_ref)` uniqueness, and a dedicated `ResolvePendingTaxClassification` command that never re-derives a `TaxInvoice` outside `IssueTaxInvoice`'s own idempotency | The pre-FIX.1 pack described this mechanism only in prose ("a queryable list of pairs") with no owning table, command, or state model — brief §11's own test ("prose without durable model/command/state") was met exactly; two implementers would have built two different, non-interoperable mechanisms | `B10_STORAGE_MODEL.md`, `B10_TAX_APPLICABILITY_MODEL.md` §3, `B10_COMMAND_EVENT_CATALOG.md` §1 | `AT-B10BL-1`…`6` |
