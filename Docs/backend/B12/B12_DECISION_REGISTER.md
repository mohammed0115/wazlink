# B12 — Decision Register

> `CLASS_A_UNRESOLVED = 0`. Every Class A decision below has an enforcement artifact and acceptance evidence. Two competent engineers reading this register plus the documents it cites should derive identical behavior without inventing a rule.

## Class A — must be resolved before implementation

| ID | Question | Decision | Reason | Enforcement artifact | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `B12-D-A001` | How many layers does the async path have? | **Six**, never collapsed: business command · durable intent · outbox event · broker delivery · worker execution · provider attempt. Only layer 4 (the broker) may be lost | Collapsing any two produces one of the four classic defects: lost intent, duplicate effect, double provider call, or a guessed outcome | `B12_ASYNC_EXECUTION_MODEL.md` §1-§3 | `AT-B12ASY-1`…`4` |
| `B12-D-A002` | May B12 write a domain table? | **Never, by any path.** Every domain effect is caused by invoking that domain's own guarded command | The frozen `Jobs` row's forbidden coupling is literally *"no domain ownership"*, and `BACKEND_INTEGRATION_BOUNDARIES.md` says provider callbacks *"never directly mutate business aggregates outside an application service"* | `B12_DOMAIN_FIREWALLS.md` (whole) | `AT-B12FW-1`…`11` |
| `B12-D-A003` | Is a Celery task ID or retry counter ever a business fact? | **No.** Both are layer-4 artifacts of a store that may be flushed | A business identity that disappears on a Redis restart is not an identity; a business budget enforced by Celery could be reset by an infrastructure event | `B12_ASYNC_EXECUTION_MODEL.md` §3 | `AT-B12CEL-6`, `AT-B12RTY-7` |
| `B12-D-A004` | What delivery guarantee does B12 claim? | **At-least-once**, explicitly. | `B12-D-A005` | May B12 make a frozen synchronous path asynchronous? | **No.** B9's and B11's write paths stay synchronous; B7 gets no wakeup sweep | Three frozen phases state this from their own side; overriding any would be B12 redesigning a closed domain | `B12_ASYNC_EXECUTION_MODEL.md` §6 | `AT-B12FW-1`, `AT-B12FW-8` |
